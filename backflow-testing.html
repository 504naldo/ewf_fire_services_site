Frequently Asked Questions

What information should I include in a backflow testing request?
Include the property address and municipality, the number of assemblies if known, their locations, and any available device details or previous test records. Mention access restrictions and your preferred timing.
Can testing affect water service?
Testing arrangements depend on the assembly and the system it serves. Tell EWF about tenant operating hours and critical water uses so access and any service interruption arrangements can be reviewed before scheduling.
Who submits the test documentation?
Include any notice or form you have received with your service details. Confirm the documentation needed and who will submit it when agreeing the work scope; submission arrangements are not assumed.
